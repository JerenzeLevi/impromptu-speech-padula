HARRY POTTER GIF PROMPTS (10 relics for the LED wall boxes)
=============================================================

HOW TO USE
- Paste each prompt into your AI generator (Veo, Runway, Pika, Kling, Luma, Midjourney video, etc.).
- Save as 1.gif ... 10.gif in assets/gifs/ (number matches the prompt).
- If the tool outputs MP4, convert to GIF (ezgif.com or ffmpeg). Keep ~3-5 sec, seamless loop.
- Tip: ask for a BLACK or very dark plain background so the box colour glows through
  (use ezgif "remove background" / make black transparent if you want it fully transparent).

GLOBAL STYLE (add to the end of EVERY prompt)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
"Single centered object, square 1:1 framing, object fills about 70% of the frame, pure black
background, cinematic dramatic lighting, warm golden rim light, magical floating dust motes and
tiny gold sparkles, rich detail, photorealistic fantasy render in the style of the Harry Potter
films, seamless 4-second loop, slow smooth camera, no text, no watermark, no people, no faces."

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. SORTING HAT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
An ancient, patched and frayed brown leather Sorting Hat with a tall crooked pointed tip, deep
wrinkles and stitched seams, floating and slowly rotating. The brim folds open and closed like
a mouth as if it is whispering, the tip gently sways. Warm candlelight glow, drifting dust,
faint amber sparkles falling from the brim.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELDER WAND
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A long, pale, knotted wooden wand with ornate carved nodules along the handle, hovering
diagonally and slowly spinning. The tip ignites with a bright white-blue spark that bursts
into swirling ribbons of golden and silver magic, curling in the air like a spell being cast,
then fades and repeats.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. GOLDEN SNITCH
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A small gleaming golden Snitch ball with delicate, fluttering silver wings engraved with
fine feather detail. It darts left and right in small quick zig-zags, hovers, then zips
again, leaving a faint glowing golden trail of light. Polished metal reflections, tiny
sparkles at each turn.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVISIBILITY CLOAK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A shimmering, silvery-silk cloak floating upright in mid-air as if worn by an invisible
person. The fabric flows like liquid mercury, rippling and swirling slowly, edges dissolving
into glittering silver mist and fading in and out of transparency. Subtle moonlit blue-silver
glow, twinkling star-like particles woven in the cloth.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOGWARTS HOUSE SCARF & ROBE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A Gryffindor scarf in crimson red and gold stripes with tassel ends, floating over a folded
black wizard robe with a small crest on the chest. The scarf waves gently as if in a warm
breeze from an unseen fire, the stitched crest glints, embers drift upward past it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. HEDWIG'S OWL FEATHER & LETTER (HOGWARTS ACCEPTANCE LETTER)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A cream parchment envelope sealed with a red wax crest, hovering and slowly spinning, with
a single white snowy-owl feather drifting around it. The envelope trembles and flutters
like it is alive, the wax seal glows warmly, handwritten emerald-green ink glimmers along
the edge. Soft white feathers and sparkles float past.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. POTION BOTTLE (POLYJUICE / FELIX FELICIS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A round glass corked flask filled with glowing liquid gold (Felix Felicis) that swirls and
bubbles inside, the liquid sloshing and releasing tiny golden bubbles and wisps of glittering
vapor from the top. The glass catches reflections, the whole bottle slowly bobs and turns,
warm amber light pulsing from within.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE MARAUDER'S MAP
-----------------------------------------------------------------
An aged, folded parchment map slowly unfolding itself in the air. Fine ink lines of corridors
and rooms draw themselves across the paper, with tiny footprints walking along the paths,
glowing faint gold. Sepia paper texture, worn creases, dark ink flourishes spreading out
and then folding back to loop.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. NIMBUS 2000 BROOMSTICK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A sleek polished mahogany Nimbus 2000 broomstick with neat golden bristles and a gold
engraved name, flying diagonally through the frame as if mid-race. Bristles ripple in the
wind, trailing glowing golden sparks and light streaks, the broom gently tilting and
banking, then looping seamlessly back.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. HARRY'S ROUND GLASSES & LIGHTNING BOLT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A pair of round black-wire glasses floating, slightly taped at the bridge, lens reflecting
a flickering torch glow. Behind them a glowing lightning-bolt scar-shaped symbol in gold
energy pulses and crackles with small electric sparks, while the glasses slowly tilt left
and right. Cozy, nostalgic, mystical tone.
